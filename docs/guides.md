# Player Guides

Guides for players of the **Pick Up Your Lantern** mod for Noita: what the mod changes in the game and how to set it up.

- [Lanterns](guides/lanterns.md): which lanterns you can pick up, holding, the belt lantern, throwing and knocking lanterns down.
- [Lantern Quality and States](guides/lantern-quality-and-states.md): lantern types, quality, rare modifiers, and what oil and water do to a lantern.
- [Vision and Darkness](guides/vision-and-darkness.md): limited vision, light sources in the darkness, polymorph and the All-Seeing Eye.
- [Settings](guides/settings.md): every Mod Settings option with its values and defaults.
