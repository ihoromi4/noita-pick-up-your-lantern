[h1]Noita, Pick Up Your Lantern![/h1]

The lanterns, lamps and torches that hang in the caves of Noita are no longer just decoration. Take them down, carry them through the dark, leave them behind to mark your way, or throw them at whatever waits in the shadows.

Out of the box, you see as in the normal game. To make the depths truly dark, pick a darker preset in Mod Settings. For a first run, try [b]Atmospheric[/b].

[h2]What the Mod Changes[/h2]
In the dark, the caves close in around you. You see only what light reveals, and every glowing thing starts to matter. A lantern becomes a light to carry, a lure to throw and a beacon to leave behind.

Look closely before you grab the first light you see. Lights differ in brightness and sturdiness, and some of them hide quirks of their own. And you are not the only one who notices the light. Some creatures are drawn to it, while others would rather stay in the dark. The rest is yours to discover.

[h2]How to Use[/h2]
[list]
[*] Walk up to a light and press the interaction key ([b]E[/b] by default) to pick it up.
[*] A light takes one item slot, like a flask.
[*] Select it to hold it in your hand. Select a wand, and the light moves to your belt, where it shines more softly.
[*] The right mouse button throws the light. The left mouse button hangs it from the ceiling above you.
[*] You can pick up a thrown or hung light again, but a hard hit may break it for good.
[/list]

[h2]Choose Your Darkness[/h2]
Pick a preset in Mod Settings. Each preset sets the vision and lantern options at once:
[list]
[*][b]Light Touch[/b]: vision as in the normal game. You can still carry and throw lights, but all lanterns are of the same quality. This is the default.
[*][b]Atmospheric[/b]: darker caves, and lanterns vary in quality.
[*][b]Survival[/b]: without a light, you see only a few steps around you.
[*][b]Pitch Black[/b]: total darkness. Without a light, you see nothing at all.
[/list]
The quality of a lantern sets how far its light reaches. The deeper you go, the better lanterns you find.

You can change the preset in the middle of a run, and the darkness changes at once. Lantern quality takes effect starting from your next run.

Two optional modes are off by default, and presets do not turn them on:
[list]
[*][b]Natural Fear[/b]: creatures fear or seek the light, each according to its nature.
[*][b]Enemy Study[/b]: watch enemies in the light to deal more damage to them.
[/list]

[h2]Documentation[/h2]
Want to know exactly how every light and creature behaves, or what each setting does? Read the detailed [url=https://github.com/ihoromi4/noita-pick-up-your-lantern/blob/main/docs/index.md]documentation[/url] on GitHub. Be warned: it spoils the surprises.

[h2]Languages[/h2]
English, Russian, Polish, Simplified Chinese.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
The mod replaces the vanilla files of lanterns and other lights, and it changes how the game draws light and darkness. Other mods that change the same things may conflict with it.
[list]
[*][b]Light-Up Lanterns[/b] is not compatible. Both mods change the same lanterns, so please enable only one of them.
[*][b]Apotheosis[/b] works together with this mod in any load order.
[/list]
