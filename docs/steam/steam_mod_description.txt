[h1]Noita, Pick Up Your Lantern![/h1]

Those oil lanterns hanging from the ceilings of the Mines are no longer just decoration. Kick them down, pick them up, carry them through the darkness, and throw them at anything that lurks in it.

[h2]Features[/h2]

[h3]Pick Up and Carry[/h3]
[list]
[*] Pick up a lantern with the interaction key ([b]E[/b]) when standing next to it.
[*] Lanterns go into your regular item slots alongside flasks and stones (one lantern per slot, no stacking).
[*] Custom inventory icons with a glowing flame.
[*] You cannot drink the oil out of a lantern. Please don't try.
[*] The lantern lights up the area around you.
[*] Holding a lantern is safe: it will not burn you, ignite the ground, or break in your hand.
[*] Throw a lantern with the [b]Left[/b] or [b]Right Mouse Button[/b], or drop it from the inventory.
[*] On a hard impact the glass shatters with a small explosion, spilling burning oil.
[*] Lanterns and world light sources clear the fog of war around them - both hanging in the world and held in your hand.
[*] Adjustable size of the illuminated area: 100%, 200%, 300% or 500%.
[/list]

[h3]Lantern on Your Belt[/h3]
Your hands are busy with a wand or a flask? Your lantern stays with you anyway.
[list]
[*] The first lantern in your inventory hangs on your belt and sways as you move.
[*] It softly lights the way - dimmer than a lantern in your hand, but enough not to stumble in the dark.
[*] Take any lantern in your hand, and the belt lantern steps aside.
[*] Adjust its brightness in the settings, from [b]Off[/b] to as bright as in hand.
[/list]

[h3]Every Lantern Is Different[/h3]
Look closely before you grab the first lantern you see!
[list]
[*] The large Lantern shines wider and is sturdier than the fragile Small Lantern.
[*] Lanterns come in four qualities, from a soot-clouded lamp to a masterwork that drives the darkness far away. The deeper you go, the better lanterns you find.
[*] Rarely a lantern has a quirk: a [b]Flickering[/b] one shines wider but wavers, and a [b]Short-lived[/b] one leaks oil and slowly burns out for good.
[*] Dive into oil with a lantern in your hand, and it blazes wider. A swim in water leaves it dim until you find oil again.
[*] The item description tells you everything about the lantern you carry.
[*] Prefer all lanterns to be equal? Turn lantern quality and quirks off in the settings.
[/list]

[h3]Player Vision and Darkness[/h3]
Make the depths truly dark and turn your lantern into a survival tool!
[list]
[*] Player vision presets: [b]Vanilla (100%)[/b], [b]Dim (75%)[/b], [b]Dark (50%)[/b], [b]Very Dark (25%)[/b], [b]Minimal (10%)[/b] and [b]Pitch Black (0%)[/b].
[*] Lower settings shrink both your personal light and the area of the map you reveal.
[*] [b]Pitch Black[/b] mode leaves unlit caves completely dark - you only see what your lantern, fires and other light sources illuminate.
[*] Fire, lava, molten metals, toxic sludge, acid and sparks glow more brightly, and burning creatures light up their surroundings.
[*] The darkness stays with you while polymorphed, and you can pick a separate vision for your polymorphed form.
[*] All-Seeing Eye, Sunseed and Sunstone reveal the world just like in the vanilla game.
[*] Vision can be changed at any time from the pause menu, no restart required.
[/list]

[h2]Languages[/h2]
English, Russian, Polish, Simplified Chinese.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
The mod replaces vanilla lantern entities and modifies shader files, so mods that change the same files may conflict. Unfortunately, the widely beloved “Light-Up Lanterns” is incompatible.
