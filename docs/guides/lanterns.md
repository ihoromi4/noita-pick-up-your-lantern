# Lanterns

Hanging oil lanterns are no longer just decoration: you can take them down, carry them, light your way and throw them.

## Which Lanterns Can Be Picked Up

- **Small Lantern**: the most common lantern on a chain, found in the Mines, Coal Pits and most other biomes.
- **Lantern**: a larger glass lantern with a stronger light, found in liquid caves and towns.

Chain torches, torch stands, mining lamps and other light sources stay in place, but they also dispel the darkness around them. See [Vision and Darkness](vision-and-darkness.md).

## Picking Up

Walk up to a hanging or fallen lantern and press the interaction key (`E` by default).

- A lantern takes one Quick Inventory slot, the same slots used by flasks and other items. Lanterns do not stack.
- If your inventory is full, nothing happens.
- You cannot drink the oil inside a lantern.
- Picked-up lanterns do not come back when you leave the area and return.

## Holding a Lantern

Select a lantern in your inventory to hold it in your hand.

- It lights up the area around you in every direction and clears the fog of war.
- It swings as you walk and fly, and settles when you stop.
- A lantern in your hand is safe: it never sets you, wooden structures, gases or liquids on fire, and it does not break while you hold it.

## Lantern on Your Belt

When your hands are busy with a wand, a flask or another item, the first lantern in your Quick Inventory hangs on your belt.

- It gives off a softer light than a lantern in hand. You can change its brightness, or turn it off, with the `Belt lantern light` setting.
- It sways with your movement.
- As soon as you take any lantern in hand, the belt lantern disappears.
- A lantern that burns out on your belt shows as a dark lantern and gives no light.

## Throwing

Throw a held lantern with the right mouse button. It flies along the dotted aiming line like any other thrown item.

> [!NOTE]
> The left mouse button also throws a held lantern. The game does not allow limiting throwing to the right button only.

- A thrown lantern keeps burning where it lands.
- A hard hit shatters it: it explodes, spills burning oil and sets the area on fire. Enemies it hits on the way take damage.
- After a soft landing it stays intact, and you can pick it up or kick it again.

## Knocking Lanterns Down

- **Kick** a hanging lantern to tear it off the ceiling.
- **Destroy the ceiling** above a lantern, and it falls down.
