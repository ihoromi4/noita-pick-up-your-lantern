# Lantern Quality and States

No two lanterns are quite alike. Each lantern has its own light, quality and sometimes a quirk, and it reacts to the liquids you dive into. Read the item description in your inventory to learn what kind of lantern you are carrying.

## Lantern Types

| Lantern | Light | Durability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lantern | Wider, warm white | Sturdier |
| Small Lantern | Smaller, warm yellow | Fragile |

## Quality

Every lantern has one of four quality levels, from a soot-clouded lantern to a masterwork one. Quality changes how far its light reaches and how much of the darkness it clears:

| Quality | Light |
| --- | --- |
| Dim | 75% |
| Ordinary | 100% |
| Bright | 150% |
| Masterwork | 200% |

The deeper you go, the better lanterns you find. In the Mines most lanterns are dim or ordinary, while masterwork lanterns start to appear from the Coal Pits and become common past the Vault.

## Rare Modifiers

About one lantern in seven has a modifier:

- **Flickering**: its flame leaps higher, so the light is 20% wider, but it wavers and sometimes dips deeply.
- **Short-lived**: oil leaks through a crack, one drop every 5 seconds. It burns for only a few minutes, slowly fading, then sputters and goes out for good. A burnt-out lantern gives no light, but you can still throw it. It only burns while lit: in your hand, on your belt or in the world, not inside your inventory.

A lantern has at most one modifier.

## Oil and Water

Dive completely under a liquid while holding a lantern, and the lantern changes:

- **Oil-soaked**: after a dive into oil, its light and the cleared area are 30% wider.
- **Wet**: after a dive into water, salt water or swamp, its light and the cleared area are 20% narrower.

A state lasts until you dive with the lantern into the opposite liquid. It stays with the lantern in your inventory, on your belt and after a throw. A burnt-out lantern does not change.

## Item Description

The description of a lantern tells its story: one sentence for its quality, and one more for each modifier or liquid state it has. The description updates as soon as the lantern changes, for example when it gets wet or burns out.

The `Lantern progression` setting turns quality and modifiers off. All lanterns are then ordinary and have no modifiers. Oil and water states still work.
