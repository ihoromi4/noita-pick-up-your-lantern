# Vision and Darkness

The mod can make the underground truly dark, so a lantern becomes something you need, not just something nice to have.

## Limited Vision

The `Player vision` setting controls how far you see underground without a lantern:

| Preset | What it feels like |
| --- | --- |
| Vanilla (100%) | The usual game. Default. |
| Dim (75%) | Slightly darker, comfortable exploring. |
| Dark (50%) | Noticeably narrower; a lantern gives a clear advantage. |
| Very Dark (25%) | Deep darkness; you see only nearby. |
| Minimal (10%) | A faint glow around your feet. |
| Pitch Black (0%) | Total darkness; you see nothing without a light source. |

- Limited vision applies only underground. The surface stays as in vanilla.
- Areas you explored earlier fall back into darkness once you leave them, even after loading a save.
- The setting applies immediately, without restarting the run.

## Light Sources in the Darkness

Light cuts through the darkness no matter how far it is from you:

- your held lantern, the lantern on your belt and lanterns you threw;
- hanging lanterns, chain torches, torch stands, mining lamps, temple lanterns, tube lamps and burning barrels;
- fire, burning oil, lava, molten metal, acid, radioactive liquid, plasma and sparks, which glow brighter than in vanilla;
- burning creatures, including you, which light up the cave around them;
- fire creatures, such as Stendari, Liekkiö and Helvetinkatse, which glow much brighter than other enemies and dispel the fog of war around them, so you can spot them from afar;
- explosions.

The `Illuminate the darkness` setting controls whether lanterns and other light sources clear the fog of war around them. The `Illuminated area size` setting makes their light reach further.

## Polymorph

Limited vision also works while you are polymorphed into another creature, and it comes back as it was when you return to your own form.

The `Polymorphed vision` setting picks how far you see while polymorphed. By default it is the same as your normal vision.

## All-Seeing Eye, Sunseed and Sunstone

- **All-Seeing Eye spell**: reveals a slowly growing circle around the place where you cast it, as bright as in vanilla. Once the circle covers the screen, the revealed area fades back into darkness over 10 seconds. Casting it again brightens the circle back up without flickering.
- **All-Seeing Eye perk**: reveals the whole level, as in vanilla.
- **Sunseed and Sunstone**: holding one reveals the whole level, the same as the perk.
